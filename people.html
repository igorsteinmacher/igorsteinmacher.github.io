Prospective Students

In my group (Computer Science / Informatics and Computing, NAU–SICCS), PhD students focus on open source sustainability, human aspects of software engineering, or AI in software development. Admission is through NAU's PhD in Informatics and Computing. When you email me, explicitly mention the research topics you're interested in and show what you have already done related to them.
